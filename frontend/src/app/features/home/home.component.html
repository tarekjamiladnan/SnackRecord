How It Works

Getting your team’s snack records organized takes only a few simple steps.

  1. 1

    Create Your Team

    Set up your team and create your record.

  2. 2

    Add Your Members

    Add everyone who participates in the team’s snack system.

  3. 3

    Track Daily Activity

    Members place their food orders while the admin records daily usage and payments.

  4. 4

    Manage the Month

    Keep daily records organized throughout the month and manage the team’s snack activity.

  5. 5

    Get Your Monthly Report

    Close the month and view everyone’s final statement with balances carried forward for the next month.
